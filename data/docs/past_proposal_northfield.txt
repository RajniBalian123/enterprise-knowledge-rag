Past Proposal — Safety Audit, Northfield Power Station (2025)

Scope
Northfield Power Station asked for a safety audit of its confined space program, covering six tanks, four vessels, and the underground utility vaults. The audit included a document review, a site walkdown, interviews with twelve operators, and a review of the last three years of entry permits.

Approach
Our team of three senior engineers spent four days on site. We reviewed the entry permit process, observed two live entries, tested the rescue plan with a tabletop exercise, and sampled atmospheric testing records against the procedure.

Findings
We found the permit process sound but the rescue plan untested: the named rescue team had not run a drill in fourteen months. Atmospheric testing records were complete, but two monitors were past their calibration date. We recommended quarterly rescue drills and a calibration tracking log.

Outcome
Northfield accepted all nine recommendations. The rescue drill program started within a month, and the follow-up audit six months later found all nine closed. The client has since engaged us for two further audits.

Team
The engagement was led by a senior safety engineer with fifteen years of confined space experience, supported by a mechanical engineer and an electrical engineer.
