Capability Statement — Harborview Engineering

Who we are
Harborview Engineering is an engineering consultancy delivering design, safety, and asset integrity services to energy and industrial clients. We have completed more than 240 projects across power generation, oil and gas, and manufacturing.

What we do
Our services include mechanical and electrical design, safety audits and hazard assessments, asset integrity management, and commissioning support. Our engineers hold professional designations and our safety record is tracked on every project.

Relevant experience
In the past three years we completed safety audits for three power plants and two refineries, including confined space program reviews, hazard and operability studies, and lockout-tagout program design. Our audit teams average twelve years of field experience.

Approach
Every engagement starts with a document review and a site walkdown. Findings are ranked by risk, and recommendations include costed remedial actions with owners and dates. We deliver a draft report within ten working days of the walkdown.

Why clients choose us
Clients choose us for senior engineers on every engagement, a fixed-fee option for defined scopes, and a record of delivering audit reports on schedule. Our repeat-client rate over the past five years is 78%.
