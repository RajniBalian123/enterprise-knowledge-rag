Travel and Expenses Policy — Harborview Engineering

Purpose
This policy sets out how employees claim travel and business expenses, and what is reimbursable.

Booking travel
Employees book travel through the approved travel provider. Flights are economy class for trips under five hours. Hotels are booked at the standard corporate rate. Any exception needs a manager's approval before booking.

Expense claims
Claims are submitted within ten working days of the trip, with receipts attached for every item over twenty-five dollars. Meals are reimbursed up to sixty dollars per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Mileage
Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the current company mileage rate, logged with origin, destination, and purpose. Parking and tolls are reimbursable with receipts.

Approval
The employee's manager approves the claim. Claims over one thousand dollars need a second approval from the department head. Finance processes approved claims within five working days.
